# Current Research Auditable Dossier Contract

`current_research_auditable_dossier` packages an already accepted structured synthesis and the existing claim-provenance trace into deterministic JSON and Markdown. It invokes the trace builder once; it does not build a second synthesis or evidence-validation path.

The dossier keeps thesis, counter-thesis, catalysts, risks, invalidation, unresolved questions, and authority limitations exactly from the accepted synthesis. Current deterministic state is a compact presentation of existing source-contract fields. Evidence-Bound Bear/Base/Bull and Research Scenario Axis CONSERVATIVE/BASE/SPECULATIVE are displayed separately.

An unaccepted response yields `REJECTED_UNTRACEABLE` and is not displayed as supported research. The dossier is presentation-only: `LEGACY_DIRECT` remains the default, packet promotion remains owner approval, and no recommendation, probability, valuation, sizing, execution, PIT, RAW_AS_TRADED, or backtest authority is created.
