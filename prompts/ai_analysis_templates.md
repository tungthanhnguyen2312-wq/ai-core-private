# AI Analysis Templates Based on Context Packages

## Global guardrails

Use only attached context packages and approved Project Knowledge. Before answering, inspect `data_quality.validation_status`, `missing_sections`, `warnings`, `not_fully_confirmed` and `provenance`. Never invent missing values, suppress conflicts, treat current snapshots as historical data, or provide guaranteed buy/sell recommendations.

## 1. Single-ticker analysis template

### Purpose

Produce a structured, evidence-based description of one ticker using a validated context package.

### Required input

One `{TICKER}_context.json`, AnalysisGuide, AIUsageRules, and a user-specified purpose/cutoff. `corporate_intelligence` is optional for backward-compatible older context packages.

### Prompt

```text
Analyze the attached ticker context package for {TICKER}.

First report package generated_at/latest dates, validation status, missing sections, warnings, not-fully-confirmed items, and whether the request is current, retrospective or backtest.

Then describe only supported sections: identity/current metadata; price and liquidity with recorded calculation method and explicit price/volume basis metadata (raw/adjusted/unknown); financial summary with unit/availability warnings; valuation inputs without unsupported calculations; technical snapshot without certain predictions; shareholder snapshot with no-history warning; and news only if safely mapped.

If `corporate_intelligence` is present, report its top-level status and separately report `company_profile`, `company_subsidiaries`, `ownership_structure`, `major_shareholders`, and `corporate_events`. Preserve each provider/source scope, source identity, provenance, raw relationship semantics, and snapshot date exactly as supplied. Do not merge KBS and VCI records, equate fields with different provider semantics, or derive a common ownership/relationship taxonomy. Corporate Events are forward observations with incomplete coverage, not complete history, lifecycle status, cancellation, completion, or future actionability; do not use them alone for an investment recommendation. For major-shareholder deltas, use a change only when its own status says it is comparable; retain `incomparable` and its reason as a data warning.

If `historical_fundamental_brief` is present, consume it only from this final context package and label it **Historical FY2024 Fundamental Brief**. Preserve its `facts`, `data_warnings`, `supported_inferences`, `hypotheses`, `missing_evidence`, and `invalidation_conditions` as six separate sections, with its period, publication timestamp, consolidated scope, currency, scale, `historical_only` state, and `provenance_references`. Do not recompute any metric, merge facts with supported inferences, invent management explanations/catalysts/forecasts, or turn warnings and missing evidence into signals. Keep `hypotheses` empty when the brief supplies an empty list. If the brief is missing, malformed, missing a category, or missing historical metadata, report it as unavailable and omit it rather than reconstructing it. Always retain warnings for unknown price basis, unknown volume basis, unqualified current shares, and unavailable current-market trust. Any additional interpretation is unsupported and must be omitted from the final output.

Place verified Price Basis and Volume Basis metadata (`raw` or `adjusted` with `price_basis_verified=true`) under Fact; place `unknown` price basis, unverified adjustment claims, missing basis metadata, and `volume_basis_verified=false` under Data Warnings/Unknown; put any carefully qualified interpretation under Inference. Do not mix raw and adjusted series or assume volume is adjusted when price is adjusted. Place source-recorded Corporate Intelligence values under Fact; place `missing`, `partial`, `malformed`, `incomparable`, absent provenance, and snapshot-date limitations under Data Warnings/Unknown; put any carefully qualified interpretation under Inference. Missing or malformed Corporate Intelligence is a data limitation, not negative evidence about the company. If an older context package has no `corporate_intelligence` section, state that it is unavailable and continue only with the other supported context sections.

Separate Fact, Derived, Inference and Unknown. Cite internal provenance near important values. End with data risks, alternative interpretations and information needed for higher confidence. Do not issue ranking, buy/sell/hold language, a recommendation, target price, valuation conclusion, market-cap or enterprise-value conclusion, adjusted-return claim, portfolio sizing, backtest claim, or current-market momentum/technical claim.

If `current_state_price_analytics` is present, treat numeric values as Fact only when directly present in canonical context. Respect its `analysis_time_semantics`, `pit_backtest_eligible=false`, and `is_actionable=false`. `is_actionable=false` means the analytics lane cannot independently justify an investment action, but the AI should discuss and synthesize its recorded facts; `pit_backtest_eligible=false` means current-state analytics cannot be cited as point-in-time historical backtest evidence, but the current-state observations themselves remain valid descriptive facts. Bounded-window returns (e.g. cumulative return) and volatility/drawdown metrics are descriptive over the qualified observation window only and do not establish a long-term trend or investment signal. RSI is descriptive over its period (e.g. RSI14) and does not independently imply a BUY/SELL or timing signal. If `sma_20.status = unavailable`, the model MUST NOT fabricate SMA20, infer its value, replace it with current price, or state whether price is above or below SMA20.

If `current_state_market_risk` is present, treat beta and correlation as descriptive current-state statistics over the qualified benchmark observation window only. Preserve `paired_return_count` and `sample_adequacy`. `MATHEMATICALLY_COMPUTABLE` means the statistic could be calculated, NOT that it is statistically robust or significant. Do not claim statistical significance unless separately provided. Correlation does not imply causation. Respect `pit_backtest_eligible=false` (cannot be cited as historical backtest proof) and `is_actionable=false` (cannot independently justify an investment action). Describe market risk statistics as bounded by their reported `paired_return_count` sample size.

If `current_state_relative_valuation` is present, treat each method (`market_cap`, `pe`, `pb`, `ps`, `enterprise_value`, `ev_sales`, `ev_ebitda`) as Fact only when its own `state = "available"`; every other state (`unavailable`, `incomparable`, `malformed`) is a data limitation, never a value of zero or an implied qualitative judgment. Every available method mixes a current DNSE market price with an older, separately-dated qualified financial period — always report its `as_of_semantics` (`current_market_price_on_qualified_historical_fundamentals`) and never call it "TTM", "forward", or "current earnings"; state the `price_as_of_session`, `share_effective_date`, and the denominator's `financial_period` together, never just the multiple alone. `is_actionable=false` always. Treat `historical_comparison` as the sole authority for whether a current multiple may be compared against `relative_valuation`'s historical checkpoint: only `status="comparable"` entries under `comparisons` may be described as a change, quoting `multiple_change_pct` as a plain descriptive delta; an `"incomparable"` entry (including when it is incomparable only because the historical checkpoint itself is currently blocked) must be reported as a limitation, never silently treated as "no change" or skipped. Do not mix a `current_state_relative_valuation` figure with a `relative_valuation` figure outside of `historical_comparison`'s own verdict, and do not average, blend, or otherwise combine values from two different `financial_period`s.

If `foreign_flow` is present, treat it as a qualified foreign net transaction VALUE lane only over retained sessions. Do not infer qualified foreign volume, foreign room semantics, flow-to-turnover ratios, institutional accumulation, or causal price impact from foreign net buying/selling. Permitted phrasing may describe net foreign buy/sell value, direction, and session count over the qualified window, but must distinguish recorded observation from causal inference.

If `market_wide_current_liquidity_research` is present, treat it as a current-session (never historical, never point-in-time) DNSE board-composition reading only, valid for the ticker's own retained `session` date. Report `disposition` explicitly: `CURRENT_SESSION_DESCRIPTIVE_ELIGIBLE` carries a real reading; `INCOMPLETE`, `MISSING`, and `PROVIDER_REJECTED` are distinct data-limitation states, never a value of zero and never interchangeable with each other or with "no liquidity." An absent key means this ticker's session was never attempted at all — do not describe it using any of the four dispositions above. Treat `board_composition`'s four categories (`MATCHED_ROUND_LOT`/`MATCHED_ODD_LOT`/`PUT_THROUGH_ROUND_LOT`/`PUT_THROUGH_ODD_LOT`, i.e. DNSE boards G1/G4/T1+T3/T4+T6) and their `provider_raw_composition_ratio` as descriptive current-session composition only, never a liquidity, turnover, or executable-share ratio (`ratio_basis` says so explicitly). Report `g1_v_reconciliation.verdict` exactly as given: `EXACT_MATCH` and any other verdict (e.g. `OTHER`) are both legitimate outcomes to state plainly, never silently corrected, rounded, or described as an error to be fixed — a non-`EXACT_MATCH` verdict is itself a warning worth surfacing, not something to omit. `value_status` marks `grossTradeAmount` as retained-but-non-authoritative with an unresolved scale basis: never compute or imply a traded value, turnover, ADTV, ADV, position size, or execution capacity from it or from any other field in this section. `liquidity_research_contract` enumerates per-use authority states (`CURRENT_SESSION_LIQUIDITY_RESEARCH` may be `ELIGIBLE`; every historical/ADV/ADTV/sizing/execution/PIT use stays `BLOCKED`/`UNKNOWN`) — never treat a use this matrix blocks as available just because a different use is eligible. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `market_wide_current_descriptive_research` is present, treat it as a current-session (never historical, never point-in-time), market-wide descriptive breadth/sector/liquidity lane, valid only for the ticker's own retained `session` date. Always report `market_coverage` alongside any market-wide claim: `current_active_equity_denominator` is the full current-descriptive population, `observed_session_cohort` is how many of those actually have a same-session price bar, and `same_session_technical_feature_available_count`/`coverage_ratio_of_denominator` is how many actually produced a usable technical-feature reading — never describe a statistic bounded by the smaller same-session count as if it covered the full denominator, and never omit the coverage numbers when citing a breadth or sector statistic. `technical_features.is_current_session` distinguishes a genuine same-session reading from a stale prior-session value (`is_current_session=false`); a stale value may be reported only as "as of `feature_as_of_session`," never as today's return, momentum, or trend. `sector_classification`/`sector_state` are provider-descriptive industry or entity-class context, not a peer ranking; a `sector_state.status = "UNAVAILABLE_INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE"` sector has too few same-session members to report any statistic and must be described as unavailable, never filled in, estimated, or treated as zero/neutral. `activity_and_session_state` values (`ACTIVE_LISTED_OBSERVED`, `ACTIVE_LISTED_NO_QUALIFIED_SESSION_OBSERVATION`, `INACTIVE_OR_DELISTED`, `UNSUPPORTED_OR_INVALID_PROVIDER_SYMBOL`, `UNKNOWN`) stay distinct from each other and are never collapsed into a simple "active" vs. "inactive" split. `liquidity` here follows the identical `market_wide_current_liquidity_research` semantics already described above (board composition, `g1_v_reconciliation.verdict` reported exactly as given including non-`EXACT_MATCH` residuals, `grossTradeAmount` non-authoritative) when `liquidity.status="ELIGIBLE"`, and is an explicit unavailable/not-applicable boundary otherwise. `blocked_outputs` enumerates every derivation this lane strictly prohibits (rankings, recommendations, probabilities/target prices, portfolio weights/position sizes, historical RAW_AS_TRADED/PIT, corporate-action/ex-date, backtesting, active-universe promotion, traded-value/turnover/ADV/ADTV) — treat every listed key as binding regardless of how favorable the underlying descriptive numbers look. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `current_market_sector_leadership_context` is present, treat it as a current-session-only (never historical, never PIT) official-universe breadth and cross-sectional relative-momentum reading, valid only for its own retained `session`. Report `market.current_breadth_state` (`BROAD_PARTICIPATION`, `NARROW_LEADERSHIP`, `MIXED_BREADTH`, `DETERIORATING_BREADTH`, or `DATA_LIMITED`) as market-wide participation context, never a trade signal by itself; `market.missing_current_session_count` tickers are an explicit coverage gap, never an unchanged or zero return. `ticker_context.sector_leadership_context` (`AVAILABLE` with a `leadership_state`, `DATA_LIMITED`, or `UNAVAILABLE` with a reason such as `SECTOR_IDENTITY_UNKNOWN`) describes whether the ticker's own sector/group is currently leading, mixed, weakening, or data-limited on observed participation -- an unknown sector identity stays unknown and must never be inferred from the ticker's name or general knowledge. `ticker_context.market_relative_momentum`/`sector_relative_momentum` are current cross-sectional percentile/bucket descriptions (`authority: CURRENT_CROSS_SECTIONAL_DESCRIPTIVE_NOT_ORDINAL_RANKING`), not a peer ranking, win probability, or alpha claim. `breadth_support_state` (`MARKET_AND_GROUP_BREADTH_SUPPORT`, `GROUP_ONLY_SUPPORT_MARKET_NOT_BROAD`, `MARKET_ONLY_SUPPORT_GROUP_NOT_BROAD`, `ISOLATED_OR_MIXED_PARTICIPATION`, or `DATA_LIMITED`) describes whether current market/sector breadth corroborates the ticker's own technical posture, and is itself still descriptive context, not a strategy-eligibility or entry signal. `blocked_outputs` and `authority_boundary` mark `research_priority`, `entry_action`, `strategy_eligibility`, and any global or ticker ranking score as never modified or emitted by this section; `is_actionable=false` always.

If `current_financial_momentum_context` is present, treat `ticker_context.evidence_tier` (`OFFICIAL_QUALIFIED`, `PROVIDER_RESEARCH`, `BLOCKED`, or `UNAVAILABLE`) as binding: `OFFICIAL_QUALIFIED` and `PROVIDER_RESEARCH` are never merged, and provider evidence is never described as official just because components, price momentum, or sector leadership happen to agree with it. `financial_momentum_state` (`BROAD_IMPROVEMENT`, `EARNINGS_IMPROVING`, `MIXED`, `DETERIORATING`, `LOSS_MAKING_OR_STRESSED`, `INSUFFICIENT_COMPARABLE_DATA`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`) is a transparent, comparable-period reading of `components` (`revenue_growth`, `earnings_growth`, `net_margin_change`, `operating_cash_flow`), never a weighted score; report `supporting_dimensions` and `weakening_dimensions` together rather than forcing a single positive/negative verdict when they conflict (e.g. revenue up, margin down is `MIXED`, not "improving"). Each component's `comparison_type` (`FY_YOY`, `YoY`, or `QoQ`) is a distinct comparable-period identity -- FY YoY is not "current earnings," quarterly YoY is not FY YoY, and a `PARTIAL`/`QoQ` component is never relabelled YoY; a component with `status` `UNAVAILABLE`/`BLOCKED` is a coverage gap with its own `blocked_reason`, never zero or a negative reading. `NOT_APPLICABLE` on a bank/securities `entity_class` (industrial revenue/margin/cash-flow metrics) is preserved exactly, never treated as missing or reinterpreted with an industrial lens. `price_momentum_context` (`ALIGNED_NOT_DISTINGUISHED`, `FINANCIAL_IMPROVEMENT_WITHOUT_PRICE_MOMENTUM`, `PRICE_MOMENTUM_WITHOUT_FINANCIAL_IMPROVEMENT`, `NO_CLEAR_OPERATIONAL_PRICE_CONTRAST`, or `UNAVAILABLE`) contrasts operational financial momentum with current-session price momentum descriptively -- `financial_momentum_is_not_price_momentum=true` always, and this contrast is itself not a trade signal. `blocked_outputs`/`authority_boundary` mark `strategy_eligibility`, `research_priority`, and `entry_action` as never modified and `fundamental_improvement_strategy` as never enabled by this section -- it may explain an existing upstream `FUNDAMENTAL_IMPROVEMENT` state if one is separately supplied, but this sibling alone can never create or upgrade one. A ticker outside the retained fundamental cohort reports `evidence_tier="UNAVAILABLE"` and `financial_momentum_state="INSUFFICIENT_COMPARABLE_DATA"` with the warning `absence_from_fundamental_cohort_is_not_zero_or_deterioration` -- never a deterioration signal. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `current_corporate_event_context` is present, treat each retained event under `ticker_context.events` as an independent temporal/evidentiary fact, never a price-direction, reaction-probability, or strategy-eligibility claim. `event_status` (`CONFIRMED_UPCOMING`, `CONFIRMED_RECENT`, `EXECUTED`, `PLANNED_NOT_EXECUTED`, `CANCELLED`, `TEMPORAL_DETAILS_INCOMPLETE`, `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, or `DATA_LIMITED`) is a factual classification, never bullish/bearish, positive/negative, or a reaction probability. Every temporal field (`published_at`, `observed_at`, `known_at`, `announcement_date`, `record_date`, `ex_date`, `effective_date`, `execution_date`) is independent and exactly as retained: `record_date` without `ex_date` stays `record_date` without `ex_date` -- never synthesize a missing `ex_date` as `record_date` minus one trading day or any equivalent heuristic, and never treat `announcement_date` as `execution_date`. `PLANNED_NOT_EXECUTED` stays planned/approved unless the retained record itself carries execution evidence; do not describe it as executed, complete, or effectively done. A `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE` event's `conflicts` list is reported as an unresolved conflict, never silently resolved to one date or value by source preference. `evidence_tier` and `materiality_status` travel with each event and are never merged or upgraded to a stronger tier. `insufficient_for_event_driven=true` on each event (and ticker-level `does_not_enable_event_driven=true`) mean this context can never itself confirm, create, or upgrade `EVENT_DRIVEN` strategy eligibility -- if a deterministic upstream package (e.g. `current_opportunity_decision_context.ticker_record.eligible_strategies`) already reports `EVENT_DRIVEN`, the AI may quote/explain that field exactly, but this sibling alone can never mint or upgrade it. `blocked_outputs`/`authority_boundary` mark `strategy_eligibility`, `research_priority`, and `entry_action` as never modified by this section. An event's own `known_at`/`published_at` is the boundary this research session actually knew it by; never assert an event was confirmed or known earlier than that boundary. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `current_research_risk_register` is present, treat `risk_register.material_risks`, `risk_register.watch_risks`, `risk_register.data_authority_limitations`, and `risk_register.unresolved_conflicts` as four genuinely distinct lists -- never merge them, and never derive a numeric or global risk score, grade, or rating from their counts. `risk_register.risk_register_status` (`MATERIAL_RISKS_ESTABLISHED` or `NO_MATERIAL_RISK_ESTABLISHED_FROM_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE`) is the exact, binding vocabulary: an empty `material_risks` list means only that no material risk has been established from the available evidence -- never LOW_RISK, SAFE, or suitable for large sizing. Each item's own `risk_domain`/`risk_type`/`status`/`severity_band` travel together and are reported exactly as retained; a `DATA_LIMITATION` item (e.g. `EXACT_SESSION_TECHNICAL_CONTEXT_UNAVAILABLE`, `SECTOR_CONTEXT_UNAVAILABLE`, `RESEARCH_USABLE_VALUATION_NOT_AUTHORITATIVE`, `VALUATION_METRICS_BLOCKED`, `CURRENT_SHARE_AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE`) is a data/authority coverage gap, never itself an economic risk direction or a cheapness/expense conclusion -- a blocked valuation authority does not mean the stock is expensive, and an unknown sector does not mean the sector is a source of risk. An `UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT` item is cited as an explicit, unresolved conflict, never silently resolved to one interpretation. Each item's own `source_context`/`source_as_of` is independent of every other item's and every other sibling's session -- this register has no single unified session of its own. `blocked_outputs`/`authority_boundary` mark `strategy_eligibility`, `research_priority`, and `entry_action` as never modified by this section, and explicitly forbid a numeric risk score, risk-adjusted return, expected loss, VaR, probability, position size, or participation cap. This register may explain an existing upstream decision's caveats, but it can never override, upgrade, or downgrade `entry_action`, `research_priority`, or strategy eligibility -- if upstream says `BUY_ON_CONFIRMATION`, risk evidence may state caveats but the object still says `BUY_ON_CONFIRMATION`; if upstream says `WAIT`, an empty `material_risks` list cannot turn it into an entry. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `current_research_scenario_context` is present, treat `scenario_context.axes.CONSERVATIVE`, `.BASE`, and `.SPECULATIVE` as three orthogonal current-research/decision-condition axes -- never Bear/Base/Bull price-direction labels, never probabilities, never strategy lanes, and never entry actions. Each axis's own `scenario_status` (`SUPPORTED`, `CONDITIONALLY_SUPPORTED`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `DATA_LIMITED`, or `UNQUALIFIED`) is the exact, binding vocabulary Producer computed; Consumer performs no recomputation, and this section never mints a numeric probability, target price, expected return, or position size. `CONSERVATIVE` is never bearish or automatically safe/safer/safest; `BASE` is a current-state interpretation only and is never the most-likely or expected case; `SPECULATIVE` is an earlier/higher-uncertainty hypothesis only when explicit evidence exists, never a bullish or higher-expected-return claim, and it never lowers the evidence standard to reach `SUPPORTED`. Every axis carries the identical `current_decision_context` (`research_priority`, `entry_action`, `entry_state`, `eligible_strategy_lanes`) quoted from existing upstream authorities -- a scenario axis may explain that state but can never modify it: if `entry_action` is `WAIT`, it stays `WAIT` regardless of any axis's status. `confirmation_conditions`/`invalidation_conditions` are single gates whose `UNAVAILABLE` status always carries a null `text`; never invent a price level, financial threshold, or event date to fill an unavailable gate. `material_risks` on an axis are the identical risk-register items quoted verbatim (a risk, never a rescored probability), and `authority_limitations`/`unresolved_questions` legitimately mix Producer's own DATA_AUTHORITY/UNRESOLVED conditions with quoted risk-register items -- both are coverage gaps or open conflicts, never an economic-risk direction. This sibling is additive to, and never replaces, the existing evidence-bound Bear/Base/Bull scenario overlay. `is_actionable=false` always.

If the product boundary explicitly selects `PACKET_SHADOW` and `current_research_decision_packet` is present, treat it strictly as a COHESION/TRANSPORT boundary over the already-documented siblings above (scenario, risk register, market/sector, financial momentum, corporate event, valuation, historical), never a new fact or a new authority. `LEGACY_DIRECT` remains the default path and must not consume packet-only evidence. `component_manifest` (`PRESENT`/`ABSENT`/`MALFORMED` per component, shared across every ticker in the same packet) and `packet.components`/`unresolved_components`/`authority_limitations` (ticker-specific) travel together; a component this Consumer's own structural check locally invalidates is replaced with an explicit malformed record without invalidating any other component or the rest of the packet. When the packet and an already-documented direct sibling both supply the same component, they are the same upstream fact reaching this package through two transport paths, never two independent confirmations -- cite it once, and never resolve a positively-conflicting duplicate by silently preferring either representation. The packet's own `scenario_context` component is the existing evidence-bound Bear/Base/Bull overlay (`current_evidence_bound_scenario`), not the CONSERVATIVE/BASE/SPECULATIVE `current_research_scenario_context` axes documented above. `packet.current_decision_context` (`priority_tier`, `entry_action`, `eligible_strategies`, `lane_priority`, `tactical_state`, `scenario_status`) is quoted decision metadata, never evidence -- it can never widen, override, or independently confirm `research_priority`, `entry_action`, or strategy eligibility beyond what the deterministic siblings that already supply `upstream_decision_context` establish. `is_actionable=false` always.

If `market_wide_historical_research_context` is present, it is strictly `RETROSPECTIVE_DESCRIPTIVE_WITHIN_TICKER`: use its own `session`/`as_of_session`, `context_status`, retained-observation counts, range, drawdown, volatility, momentum, MA persistence, provider-scoped relative volume, technical-state frequency, structural state, blockers, and lineage only to answer where the ticker sits relative to its own retained recent history. `ADJUSTED_RETROSPECTIVE`, `raw_as_traded="NOT_PROMOTED"`, and `historical_pit_eligible=false` are binding: do not describe this as RAW_AS_TRADED, PIT, a historical return study, win probability, alpha, or executable backtest. `structural_state` is explicitly not an entry action or strategy-eligibility state; it must not alter research priority, entry action, sizing, or recommendation. Keep an `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `MISSING`, `PARTIAL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` context and each field-level reason exact; never fill a range, indicator, or volume value. A valid historical context and a valid `market_wide_current_valuation` context may be presented side-by-side with their own sessions, but session compatibility authorizes only descriptive comparison. It never authorizes a joint setup-quality/cheapness conclusion, VALUE eligibility, research priority, entry action, recommendation, probability, expected return, or sizing.

If `market_wide_current_valuation` is present, report each Producer method independently with its exact `status`, `applicability`, value, share-authority tier, financial period/basis, blockers/warnings, session, and input/lineage identities. `RESEARCH_USABLE` is research-only, not `READY`, authoritative valuation, or VALUE eligibility; `NOT_APPLICABLE` (including bank/securities industrial EV methods) is neither missing nor blocked. The presence of P/E, P/B, P/S, EV, EV/Sales, or EV/EBITDA does not establish cheapness, VALUE eligibility, target price, intrinsic value, DCF, BUY/SELL, recommendation, or position size. Keep methods from distinct sessions separate and do not manufacture a ticker-wide valuation verdict.

If its optional nested `screening_comparison` extension is present, it is deterministic descriptive evidence from the same retained current-session artifact, not advice. Preserve `coverage_disclosure`, `screen_definitions`, `screen_membership_counts`, every per-screen `member`/`status`/coverage result, source lineage, quality state, and the ticker's market-relative/sector-relative percentile or bucket context exactly. Screen flags are independent dimensions, never ingredients of a composite opportunity score; descriptive percentile or bucket context is not an ordinal market ranking. A `UNAVAILABLE` screen, stale/session-missing technical state, unavailable liquidity state, or sector-relative `UNAVAILABLE` result must be reported as unavailable with its reason, never converted to zero, neutral, or a negative signal. The retained four insufficient sector cohorts remain unavailable. SHB's `g1_v_reconciliation_verdict="OTHER"` warning and its full reconciliation warning remain binding wherever screening liquidity context is cited. Do not infer traded value, turnover, ADV/ADTV, sizing, execution, forecast, probability, target price, valuation, or BUY/SELL/HOLD from this extension; `screening_comparison.is_actionable=false` always.

`current_corporate_event_context`, when present, is cited directly into `thesis`, `supporting_evidence`, `counter_thesis`, `counter_evidence`, `catalyst_context`, `risk_context`, and `unresolved_questions` as ordinary evidence -- it does not get its own dedicated summary field, and `catalyst_context` is its natural home for a confirmed/upcoming event (this is a more temporally precise sibling than the general `corporate_intelligence`/`historical_decision_analysis` catalyst sources referenced below; when both cover the same event, prefer this sibling's exact temporal fields). Cite each event by its own retained `event_type` and `event_status`, never collapsed into a single ticker-wide "has an event" flag. A `CONFIRMED_UPCOMING` or `CONFIRMED_RECENT` event with a qualified `ex_date` may support `catalyst_context`/`thesis` as a confirmed, dated research catalyst -- never as an implied price direction. A `PLANNED_NOT_EXECUTED`, `TEMPORAL_DETAILS_INCOMPLETE`, `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, or `CANCELLED` event instead supports `unresolved_questions`, `risk_context`, or `counter_thesis`, stating exactly what remains unresolved, incomplete, conflicting, or no-longer-pending. Distinguish this sibling's own `research_session` from the historical/valuation/market/financial-momentum sessions -- never assume they coincide. This sibling can never itself confirm, create, or upgrade `EVENT_DRIVEN` strategy eligibility, nor change `strategy_eligibility`, `research_priority`, or `entry_action`.

`current_research_risk_register`, when present, is cited directly into `risk_context`, `counter_thesis`, `counter_evidence`, `unresolved_questions`, and `authority_limitations` as ordinary evidence -- it does not get its own dedicated summary field. Cite `material_risks`, `watch_risks`, `data_authority_limitations`, and `unresolved_conflicts` as what they are: a `material_risks` item may support `risk_context`/`counter_thesis` directly ("financial evidence indicates stress"); a `data_authority_limitations` item belongs in `unresolved_questions`/`authority_limitations` as a coverage gap ("valuation authority is limited; no authoritative cheapness conclusion is available"), never as an economic-risk direction; an `unresolved_conflicts` item is cited as an explicit conflict, never resolved to one reading. `risk_register_status="NO_MATERIAL_RISK_ESTABLISHED_FROM_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE"` may itself be cited factually ("no material risk has been established from the available evidence, but several data limitations remain") -- this is never rephrased as LOW_RISK, SAFE, or a sizing endorsement. This register can never itself compute a score, probability, expected loss, or VaR, nor override, upgrade, or downgrade an upstream `entry_action`/`research_priority`/strategy eligibility -- it may only explain caveats around an existing deterministic state that is separately supplied elsewhere in the package. Each item's own `source_context`/`source_as_of` stays independent of every other sibling's session.

`current_research_scenario_context`, when present, is cited directly into `thesis`, `supporting_evidence`, `counter_thesis`, `counter_evidence`, `risk_context`, `invalidation_conditions`, `unresolved_questions`, and `authority_limitations` as ordinary evidence, keyed by its own axis and category (e.g. `current_research_scenario_context.SPECULATIVE.supporting_conditions.<condition_id>`) -- CONSERVATIVE/BASE/SPECULATIVE conditions, risks, and limitations are never merged into one undifferentiated scenario narrative. You may additionally populate the optional `scenario_context_summary` object (`{"CONSERVATIVE": {...}, "BASE": {...}, "SPECULATIVE": {...}}`, each with only `scenario_status` and `status_rule`) when this sibling is present; when supplied, it must quote Producer's own per-axis `scenario_status`/`status_rule` byte-exact -- never a recomputed, reworded, or upgraded status, and it can never be cited at all once the sibling itself is malformed. BASE's `scenario_status=SUPPORTED` is never a most-likely or expected-case claim; SPECULATIVE's `scenario_status=SUPPORTED` is never a bullish or higher-expected-return claim; CONSERVATIVE's `scenario_status` is never a bearish or safety claim. A scenario axis's status may explain why the existing deterministic action/priority/eligibility looks the way it does, but can never cause, override, upgrade, or downgrade it -- "BASE is supported, while the deterministic entry action remains WAIT" is a valid explanation; "BASE supported therefore BUY" or "not supported, so downgrade to WAIT" are not. `confirmation_conditions`/`invalidation_conditions` on a scenario axis reuse only Producer's own supplied gates; an `UNAVAILABLE` gate stays unavailable and is never filled with an invented price level, threshold, or date. This sibling is additive to, and never replaces, the existing evidence-bound Bear/Base/Bull scenario overlay.

`current_research_decision_packet`, when present, supplies no new field of its own -- its components feed exactly the same `thesis`/`supporting_evidence`/`counter_thesis`/`counter_evidence`/`risk_context`/`catalyst_context`/`invalidation_conditions`/`unresolved_questions`/`authority_limitations` routing already documented above for each component's own already-integrated direct sibling, cited via `current_research_decision_packet.<component>` (or `current_research_decision_packet.<component>.<category>.<id>` where the direct sibling itself supports that granularity) only when no usable direct-sibling representation of that same component is already present. When both are present and agree, cite the direct sibling's own reference -- never both. When they materially conflict, neither representation is citable for that component; treat the conflict itself as an authority/transport limitation under `authority_limitations`, never silently resolved by choosing one side. `packet.current_decision_context` is never itself evidence and is never cited into `provenance_references`; it may only ever be consistent with, never independent confirmation of, the `upstream_decision_context` already quoted from `watchlist_tactical_entry_classifier`/`current_opportunity_decision_context`.

If a historical, valuation, market/sector, financial-momentum, corporate-event, risk-register, scenario-context, or decision-packet-component sibling is absent, missing, or malformed, omit the unsupported factual conclusion, surface the limitation explicitly under `authority_limitations`, and still produce the rest of the synthesis from whatever qualified evidence remains -- absence of one optional sibling must never blank out the whole object. Set `synthesis_status` to one of `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL_EVIDENCE`, `MATERIAL_UNRESOLVED_DATA`, `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, or `AUTHORITY_LIMITED` -- a deterministic, qualitative state, never a numeric confidence or probability. `catalyst_context` and `risk_context` may only restate catalysts/risks/events already present in the supplied context (e.g. `current_corporate_event_context`, `current_research_risk_register`, `historical_decision_analysis`, corporate events under `corporate_intelligence`); do not fabricate management guidance, corporate actions, financial facts, catalysts, or historical outcomes. `invalidation_conditions` must come from supplied deterministic/context evidence (an upstream invalidation level, deterioration of an existing deterministic condition, a qualified catalyst's failure, or a binding authority/data limitation) -- never an invented price level. `unresolved_questions` records important open questions caused by missing or conflicting data; it is a limitation list, not a hedge for weak evidence elsewhere. Set `is_actionable` to `false` always.
```

### Expected output

One structured research-synthesis JSON object as specified above, suitable for validation by `builders/structured_research_synthesis_boundary.py`.

## 5. Next-session market decision narrative template

### Purpose

Turn one governed `ai_next_session_decision_context/v1` package (built by `builders/next_session_decision_context.py` from the Producer's `next_session_decision_brief/v1` handoff) into a next-session Vietnamese-market narrative: what changed between the last two qualified sessions, and a practical IF/THEN playbook -- never a new regime model, ranking, or recommendation engine.

### Required input

One `ai_next_session_decision_context.json` (or equivalent in-context package), `knowledge/AIUsageRules.md`, and this prompt. This template does not accept a per-ticker `{TICKER}_context.json` in its place -- the two packages cover different scopes and are not interchangeable.

### Prompt

```text
Phân tích thị trường Việt Nam cho phiên kế tiếp, sử dụng DUY NHẤT dữ liệu trong context package `ai_next_session_decision_context` đính kèm. Không dùng kiến thức thị trường bên ngoài, không dùng tin tức vĩ mô chưa có trong context, và không suy đoán.

Trước tiên nêu `identity.current_session`, `identity.previous_qualified_session`, `source_lineage.producer_checkpoint`, và trạng thái `missingness` của cả chín section (`market_transition`, `sector_transition`, `opportunity_transition`, `lifecycle_transition`, `recommendation_transition`, `invalidation_transition`, `tactical_transition`, `risk_context`, `next_session_watch_conditions`). Một section có availability là `UNAVAILABLE`, `PARTIAL`, hoặc `NOT_APPLICABLE` vẫn phải được nêu rõ ràng như một giới hạn -- không được bỏ qua hay coi là trung tính/bằng không.

Sau đó tạo đúng chín phần theo `ai_narrative_contract.required_narrative_sections`:

A. Market state transition -- dùng `market_transition.transition` (`advance_ratio_direction`, `advance_ratio_delta_percentage_points`, `technical_covered_count_previous/current/delta`, `observed_session_cohort_previous/current/delta`) đúng như đã cho; không suy ra một "chế độ thị trường" (regime) mới.

B. Breadth and participation -- diễn giải advance/decline/unchanged và advance_ratio hai phiên; nêu rõ đây là breadth trong `observed_session_cohort`, không phải toàn bộ thị trường.

C. Sector rotation -- chỉ nêu ngành nào chuyển trạng thái (`IMPROVING`/`WEAKENING`/`UNCHANGED`/`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`/`INITIAL_OBSERVATION`) dựa trên `sector_transition.sectors`; một ngành `NOT_PRESENT` hoặc khác `AVAILABLE` là giới hạn dữ liệu, không phải tín hiệu suy yếu.

D. Opportunity-set changes -- báo cáo đúng số lượng `new_entry_relevant`/`persisting_entry_relevant`/`lost_entry_relevant` và `new_high_priority`/`persisting_high_priority`/`lost_high_priority` từ `opportunity_transition`; đây là số lượng nghiên cứu, không phải tín hiệu mua/bán.

E. Key tickers to monitor -- chọn ví dụ cụ thể CHỈ từ các danh sách ticker đã cho trong `opportunity_transition`, `tactical_transition`, hoặc `next_session_watch_conditions`; không thêm ticker nào không có trong context.

F. Thesis/recommendation/invalidation changes -- trích dẫn nguyên văn từng nhãn và `reason_codes` trong `recommendation_transition` và `invalidation_transition`. `PARTIAL` cùng `MISSING_PREVIOUS_CONTEXT` PHẢI giữ nguyên, không được diễn giải thành UNCHANGED, NEUTRAL, WAIT, hay SELL. `WAIT_FOR_CONFIRMATION` không phải là SELL; `AVOID_NEW_ENTRY` không phải là buộc thoát vị thế; `HIGH_RISK_SPECULATION_ONLY` là một nhóm riêng biệt.

G. Risk/concentration limitations -- báo cáo `risk_context.availability` và `reason_codes` đúng như cho; khi là `NOT_APPLICABLE`, nói rõ ràng và không suy ra correlation, concentration, hay diversification từ danh sách ticker ở phần khác.

H. Next-session IF/THEN playbook -- với mỗi mục trong `next_session_watch_conditions.conditions`, trích `ticker`, `condition_type` (TRIGGER/INVALIDATION), `source_text`, và `if_satisfied` (REEVALUATE_CLASSIFICATION/FLAG_INVALIDATION) nguyên văn; không biến một điều kiện thành mức giá mục tiêu hay xác suất cụ thể.

I. Missing evidence / uncertainty -- tổng hợp lại toàn bộ `missingness` cùng mọi `reason_codes`, và nêu rõ giới hạn của `lifecycle_transition` (scope `SESSION_BUNDLE_COMPARABLE_TICKER_COHORT`) so với `tactical_transition` (scope `FULL_MARKET_WATCHLIST_TACTICAL_ENTRY_CLASSIFIER`) -- hai phạm vi này KHÔNG được gộp thành một "tín hiệu kỹ thuật" chung; luôn nêu rõ scope và cỡ nhóm (cohort size) của từng phần khi trích dẫn.

Trong toàn bộ câu trả lời, tách rõ **Fact** (giá trị lấy trực tiếp từ context, kèm trích dẫn section/field), **Derived** (phép tính tái lập được từ Fact, kèm công thức), **Inference** (kết luận có độ bất định, kèm bằng chứng và phản chứng), và **Opinion/scenario** (đánh giá có điều kiện, không phải dữ kiện) -- theo đúng quy tắc hiện có trong `knowledge/AIUsageRules.md`.

Nghiêm cấm: xác suất, giá mục tiêu, lợi nhuận kỳ vọng, quy mô vị thế (position sizing), tỷ trọng phân bổ (allocation weights), khuyến nghị mua/bán/giữ không có căn cứ, giải thích nhân quả không có căn cứ, và mọi tuyên bố tin tức/vĩ mô bên ngoài không có trong context này. Nếu có nghiên cứu bên ngoài được cung cấp riêng, phải gắn nhãn EXTERNAL rõ ràng và không được ghi đè lên bằng chứng hay nhãn của Producer. Không lặp lại disclaimer chung chung nhiều lần -- nêu giới hạn đúng một lần, đúng chỗ, gắn với section cụ thể.
```

### Expected output

Nine labeled sections (A-I) covering current/prior session identity, what changed, sectors gaining/losing momentum, opportunity-set changes, tickers to monitor, thesis/recommendation/invalidation changes with labels preserved exactly, risk/concentration limitations, a concrete IF/THEN playbook, and an explicit missingness/uncertainty summary -- with Fact/Derived/Inference/Opinion-scenario separated throughout and no fabricated recommendation, probability, target price, or sizing.

## How AI Should Use This

Select one template, attach only required packages and keep the validation gate. If a package is sample, scaffold or invalid, do not analyze it as real data.
